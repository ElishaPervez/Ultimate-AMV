2Why the lossless export cuts the wrong length

He worked this out himself and he's right. The lossless export skips re-encoding to stay perfect and fast — but that means it can only cut at the video's built-in “anchor” frames, not at any arbitrary frame. So when the scene starts between two anchors, the cut jumps to the nearest one and sweeps in a bit of the neighbouring clip.
